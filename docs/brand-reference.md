# Kooya brand reference

Inspected 2026-10-08 at [kooya.com.ph](https://kooya.com.ph/).

Verified source: `/assets/V2-C_fFDGht.css` on that site. Its V2 palette defines
paper `#f2eee3`, ink `#13120f`, lime `#d7f20f`, deep green `#314300`, yellow
`#f4b701`, blue `#1d46d9`, and red `#ef3e2f`. A live browser shows the infinity
wordmark, DM Sans body, and Syncopate display headings.

The Kooya Signature theme translates paper, ink, and lime into product
surfaces, selected navigation, and action tokens. The soft Mosaic theme stays
available independently. The interface uses readable action text and native
cursors; cinematic backgrounds, sound, display-scale headings, and the site's
eye cursor are not part of the component library.

The local playground wordmark is copied from
`https://kooya.com.ph/brand/kooya-wordmark.png?v=infinity-2026-08` into
`apps/playground/public/brand/kooya-wordmark.png` (1150×310 PNG). Stephen
identified this as the company's brand. Its transparent lime lettering is
placed on a charcoal surface for readable contrast. Preserve aspect ratio and record a
new source/date when replacing it. Components accept a brand slot, so other
products can provide their own approved assets without depending on this file.

The bento glyph in the Mosaic sample is an app prototype mark. It does not
replace the official Kooya wordmark or establish a new corporate logo.
Fonts and the image are served locally; opening a dummy UI sample never
contacts the corporate site.
